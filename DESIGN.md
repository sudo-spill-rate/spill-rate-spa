# Design Direction: TrustRate

## Identity & Purpose
TrustRate is an authentic community review and ratings directory for local businesses and SMEs. It emphasizes transparency, verified merchant interaction, and spam-free feedback protected by device cooldowns.

## Personality & Mood
- **Character**: Trustworthy, civic-minded, human, grounded, and transparent.
- **Tone**: Professional yet approachable, honest, free of AI buzzwords and hyperbolic claims.
- **Anti-patterns to avoid**: No generic blue-purple gradients, no blurred floating background orbs, no pill-badge "AI-powered" tags, no fake statistics, no emoji decoration in headings or buttons, no dead navigation controls.

## Color Palette
- **Core Neutral Base**: Warm Slate & Crisp Paper (`#f8fafc` canvas, `#ffffff` card surfaces, `#e2e8f0` / `#cbd5e1` borders, `#0f172a` primary text, `#475569` secondary text). Meets WCAG AA contrast standards (> 4.5:1 for normal copy, > 3:1 for large text).
- **Core Brand Primary**: Deep Slate (`#0f172a` / `#1e293b`) for foundational headers, primary actions, and solid anchoring.
- **Deliberate Accent**: Forest Teal & Emerald (`#0d9488` / `#059669`) for verified trust badges, primary submission buttons, and key interactive highlights.
- **Rating Accent**: Warm Amber (`#d97706`) specifically for customer review star ratings.

## Typography
- System Sans-serif stack (`Inter`, system-ui, sans-serif) with high legibility, natural line-heights, standard sentence casing, and structured typographic scale.
- No forced uppercase letter-spacing or decorative monospace titles.

## Dials & Levers
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced, trustworthy, clear)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Consistent grid structure with narrative breaks: search hero, spotlight cards, split directory view)
- **MOTION**: 1 (Hover states and focus transitions only; no perpetual looping or bouncy animations)
- **One Deliberate Accent**: Forest Teal / Emerald used selectively for verified badges and primary action.
- **Focal Point per Screen**: Distinct, immediately identifiable main task on every screen (e.g., search bar on Landing, search & filter sidebar on Explore, inquiry form on Contact, readable accordion on FAQ).
