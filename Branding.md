# Branding & AI Generation Guide: TrustRate

This guide contains production-ready, anti-AI-slop image generation prompts and style presets tailored for ChatGPT Plus (DALL-E 3 / GPT-4o Image Generation). It focuses on the core brand assets: **Logo**, **Vector Illustrations**, **Character Mascot ("Bantay")**, and **Design Presets**.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Mascot Overview

* **Brand Name:** TrustRate
* **Core Value:** Authentic community ratings and verified reviews for local businesses and SMEs.
* **Core Promise:** Spam-free, transparent customer feedback protected by device verification and merchant cooldowns.
* **Brand Personality:** Grounded, trustworthy, civic-minded, loyal, and accessible.
* **Official Animal Mascot & Guide:** **"Bantay" The Community Guide Dog**
  * *Archetype:* The faithful guide dog and local neighborhood guardian (an intelligent, alert, friendly hound and retriever companion).
  * *Symbolic Meaning:*
    * **Guidance:** Guides customers directly to authentic, top-rated local spots.
    * **Protection:** Sniffs out fake spam reviews, coordinated troll attacks, and bots.
    * **Loyalty:** Stands proudly beside honest local entrepreneurs, bakeries, workshops, and cafes.
  * *Visual Attire:* Sleek slate and warm-toned coat, wearing a structured Forest Teal (`#0d9488`) guide vest or collar with a Warm Amber (`#d97706`) verified rating star medallion.
* **Color Palette (from DESIGN.md):**
  * **Deep Slate (Primary Anchor):** `#0f172a` / `#1e293b`
  * **Forest Teal & Emerald (Trust & Verification Accent):** `#0d9488` / `#059669`
  * **Warm Amber (Rating Star Accent):** `#d97706`
  * **Warm Slate & Paper (Backgrounds & Canvases):** `#f8fafc` / `#ffffff`
  * **Muted Slate (Borders & Structural Lines):** `#cbd5e1` / `#475569`

## 2. Master Style Preset (ChatGPT Plus Style Anchor)

Copy and paste this master preset into ChatGPT Plus before generating assets, or include it as the opening system context for your image generation thread:

```text
[STYLE ANCHOR - TRUSTRATE BRANDING]
Visual Direction: High-craft modern editorial vector illustration and minimalist flat graphic design.
Aesthetic Heritage: Clean Swiss graphic design, Monocle magazine editorial spot illustrations, thoughtful product branding.
Color Palette: Restricted palette of Deep Slate (#0f172a), Forest Teal (#0d9488), Warm Amber (#d97706), Crisp White (#ffffff), and Warm Off-White (#f8fafc).
Linework: Uniform medium-weight strokes, clean geometric precision, balanced negative space, crisp vector edges.
Strict Negative Constraints (Anti-AI-Slop):
- NO 3D clay or Pixar-style glossy rendering
- NO generic neon glows, lens flares, or floating cyber particles
- NO purple-to-blue or magenta gradients
- NO floating geometric orbs or sci-fi gridlines
- NO distorted anatomy, extra paws, or plastic skin sheen
- NO generic corporate Memphis blobs with noodle arms
- NO busy, hyper-detailed backgrounds
Composition: Clean, deliberate focal point on an off-white background (#f8fafc), vector format, flat 2D presentation.
```

## 7. ChatGPT Plus Workflow Steps (Quick Guide)

1. **Open a Fresh Chat in ChatGPT Plus (GPT-4o / DALL-E 3).**
2. **Send Section 2 (Master Style Preset)** as your first prompt to lock in the 2D editorial anti-slop guidelines.
3. **Generate the Logo First:** Run **Prompt 1A** (App Icon) or **Prompt 1B** (Bantay Emblem).
4. **Generate the Character Turnaround Sheet:** Run **Prompt 2A** to establish Bantay's proportions, coat colors, and expressions.
5. **Generate Action Vectors & Spot Illustrations:**
   * For empty states or spot art, run **Prompt 2B**, **2C**, or **3B**.
   * For stickers and badges, run **Prompt 2D** and **3A**.
   * *Tip for ChatGPT consistency:* Always mention: *"Keep the exact character design, linework, and color palette from the previous image."*
